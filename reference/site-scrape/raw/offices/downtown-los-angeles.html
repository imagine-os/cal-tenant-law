Downtown Los Angeles Tenant Lawyer

Serving tenants in the Downtown Los Angeles area.

California Tenant Law provides experienced legal representation for tenants throughout Downtown Los Angeles and the greater LA area. Founded by Kenneth H. Carlson, J.D. (CA State Bar #93602) in 1980, our practice exclusively represents tenants facing eviction, security deposit disputes, habitability issues, and landlord harassment.

Our Downtown LA office serves tenants in DTLA, Koreatown, Echo Park, Silver Lake, Hollywood, and surrounding neighborhoods. Whether you’re dealing with LA’s Rent Stabilization Ordinance, facing an unlawful eviction, or need guidance on your rights under California tenant protection laws, we provide accessible, affordable legal expertise backed by over four decades of California tenant law experience.

How We Help Downtown Los Angeles Tenants

Eviction defense and unlawful detainer cases
LA Rent Stabilization Ordinance (RSO) guidance
Ellis Act and Owner Move-In eviction defense
Security deposit recovery under California Civil Code §1950.5
Repair and habitability issues (Civil Code §1942)
Illegal rent increases and rent control violations
Landlord harassment and illegal entry (Civil Code §1954)
Tenant buyout agreement review and negotiation
Just Cause eviction protections under AB 1482

Downtown Los Angeles Areas Served

We also coordinate with our San Fernando Valley office for tenants in the greater LA metropolitan area.
