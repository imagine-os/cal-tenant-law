# P-13 · Services outline

## Purpose

The whole catalog as one collapsible outline, exactly as the store files it. P-10 is the shop window (cards, filters, one stage at a time); this is the index. Two trees behind one switch: **Store menu** (default) is what a visitor sees on caltenantlaw.com/store today: the 20 menu categories in menu order, each with its products in the order the store lists them (two levels; the live store has no visible sub-categories). **Firm's stage map** is the firm's older stage-based navigation that is still attached to every product in Ecwid but hidden from the menu: Consultation · I'm Being Evicted... · Legal Papers · Sue Your Landlord, four levels deep, a product under every stage it is filed in, plus "Store menu only" for the 41 products the old tree never filed (D-041). Every row carries the firm's own store icon, the SKU, title, price ("as listed on caltenantlaw.com on 2026-09-18 · unverified"), time expectation, what you receive and what you provide. It is the view the firm can print, mark up in pen and hand back.

## Sections (layout order)

1. **Hero** — what the outline is, and the counts (20 store categories, 98 services, 98 with a posted price, the scrape date).
2. **Tools** — the Store menu / Firm's stage map switch, expand all, collapse all, print, and the keyboard hint; the description under the title explains the tree that is showing.
3. **OutlineTree** — the tree itself (store: category → product; stages: root → ... → leaf → product).
4. **PrintNote** — the "as listed on caltenantlaw.com on 2026-09-18" line and the artwork provenance that go on the printout.

## Rules

- `RULE-CATALOG-01` — prices carry the "as listed on caltenantlaw.com on <scraped_at> · unverified" badge on every row.
- `RULE-CATALOG-02` — the off-board services sit under their own categories, never invented squares; a service with no square shows no board link.
- `RULE-CATALOG-06` — anything the store's description does not state renders "to be confirmed" with a tooltip, never a plausible default.

## Logic

- A real ARIA tree (`role="tree"` / `treeitem`, `aria-level`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-selected`) with **roving focus**: exactly one row is in the tab order, and the arrow keys move within the tree.
- **Keyboard**: ↓ / ↑ move through the rows that are actually visible; → opens a closed branch, or steps into an open one; ← closes an open branch, or steps out to the parent; Home / End jump to the first and last visible row; Enter (or Space) opens a service's detail page, or toggles a branch.
- The view is addressable: `?view=stages` in the URL (the switch writes it back, `replace`), so a link or the voice controller can open the stage map directly.
- Store menu: 20 roots open on load = 118 rows; every service appears once. Stage map: 5 roots (4 hidden roots + "Store menu only"), 233 rows fully expanded and up to five levels; a product appears under every hidden leaf it is filed in (57 products in the tree, 41 only in the menu), because that is how the store files it.
- Root categories open on first load and whenever the view switches, so the page reads as a table of contents.
- A category with no services still renders, with a count of zero: an empty shelf in the store is a fact about the store.
- Print styles drop the site header, footer, hero, toolbar, chevrons and the icons, keep every row from breaking across pages, and set the tree at 11 pt.

## Real vs mock

The tree is the real catalog scraped live from caltenantlaw.com on 2026-09-18 (`docs/data/services-catalog.json`, D-040): 98 services, every one priced or free; `time_expectation` for 69, `client_inputs` for 94, `deliverable_format` for all 98, read from each product's own store description; the rest render "to be confirmed". Icons are the firm's own store artwork (`illustrations` table, D-042; 93 of 98 services, all 20 menu categories). Nothing is confirmed by the firm yet (`verified: false`); A-10 is where that happens.

## Inputs and responsive check (P-01, P-03)

Checked at: 360, 390, 768, 1280, 1920, 2560, 3840, light and dark, in all three visual directions. Rows are 44 px minimum and wrap their metadata onto a second line on phones; indentation is `--depth × --sp-5` so it scales with `--scale` on a TV; the focused row carries both a 3 px focus ring and an accent bar. Nothing is hover-only and nothing is drag-only: every branch has a visible chevron, a click target and a keyboard path; the view switch is a `radiogroup`.
