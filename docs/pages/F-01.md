# F-01 · Front desk today

## Purpose

One screen to run the day at an office — and on the wall screen, across the whole network. What is ringing, what was missed, the callbacks due, the orders waiting on a client, what clients still owe us, the due dates this week, the consultations booked today and later this week, the intake queue and what is waiting to be paid. The front desk is where the firm's funnel actually lands: form → consultation → SKU-priced work — and, since pass 2 (prompt 0006), where a caller's order status is found in one box.

Access (D-048, 2026-09-20): `front_desk`, `owner` and `super_admin` (was every staff role). Attorneys, paralegals and marketing have their own homes; the wall-screen use stays with the desk and the owner.

## Sections (layout order)

1. PageHeader — office chip, "Open the call console", "New intake" and "Book a consultation".
2. Order status lookup — one search box; Enter goes to `/desk/orders?q=…` (F-14, read-only).
3. StatTiles (eight, each a link) — calls live now, missed today, callbacks due, waiting on a client, needed from clients, due dates this week, consultations today, intake queue.
4. ConsultationsToday — a timeline: time, client, kind, channel, attorney, price, paid state, status, "Mark as held".
5. CallsToReturn — missed and voicemail rows with the number and time; "Call back" opens F-12 on that call.
6. FollowUpsDue — the open follow-ups due today or earlier, with the order link and how late they are.
7. WaitingOnAClient — open orders whose waiting-on is the client, with the `WaitingOnPill`.
8. ConsultationsThisWeek — the next seven days.
9. IntakeQueue — DataTable with caller, received, the square they describe, office, status; "Review" and "Schedule" per row.
10. PaymentsPending — DataTable with item, SKU, client, office, amount; "Record" per row.

## Rules

- `RULE-INTAKE-01` — a consultation is a prepaid, time-boxed 30-minute block; hotline blocks are $60 per 10 minutes as listed.
- `RULE-SYS-02` — every write is by id through the provider.
- `RULE-PIPE-02` — the waiting-on pill comes from the stage, not from a field the desk edits.
- `RULE-PIPE-06` — the desk looks orders up and never advances them; the lookup box goes to the read-only F-14.

## Logic

- Rows are scoped to the signed-in office; owner and super admin are network-wide and the header says so (P-02).
- Today = `scheduled_at` falls on today; this week = the next seven days, excluding today.
- Prices show with the "as listed" caveat wherever they appear.
- Live calls = `ringing` / `active` / `on_hold`; "missed today" counts missed and voicemail rows started today; callbacks due = open follow-ups due today or earlier (`lib.isDueNow`).
- Waiting on a client = open orders whose `waitingOnFor(stage)` is `client`; needed from clients = open `client_requests`; due dates this week = `filing_due_at` or `due_at` inside seven days.
- The lookup box navigates, it never writes: `/desk/orders?q=<text>` is the desk's read-only order lookup (F-14).

## Placeholders

## Real vs mock

Real: the day's shape, the scoping, the two writes, and every pipeline count (calls, callbacks, waits, requests, dates). Mock: the rows; the scheduler and payments are seams, and the phone system behind `calls` arrives in Pass 3.

## Inputs and responsive check (P-01, P-03)

Checked at 360–3840, light and dark. At 360–390 the tables fall back to the card layout and board-square chips ellipsize (`.homes-chip`) instead of pushing the page sideways. The timeline keeps the time column at a fixed width so the eye can scan it from across the room; at 2560 and 3840 `--scale` lifts the type and the two-column band becomes three. The eight tiles are an auto-fitting grid, so the wall screen gains columns rather than whitespace and each tile stays a 44 px link.
