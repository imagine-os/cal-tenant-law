# Late fees

> **Not verified legal advice.** Written 2026-09-18 from the firm's site as indexed and general knowledge; every citation below has `verified_on: null` in `../statute-index.md`. Verify before use (see `../README.md`).

**What the firm covers.** "The most common theft by landlords is the 'Late Fee'." Late fees as unenforceable liquidated damages; the firm's reading that fees above ~10 % are presumed unreasonable; an illegal fee is a credit against rent (a defense in nonpayment cases: the 3-day notice demands the wrong amount).

**Statutes.** CC §1671 (liquidated damages in consumer contracts), CCP §1161(2) (notice must state the exact amount of rent due), Orozco v. Casimiro (2004) 121 Cal.App.4th Supp. 7 (late fees, verify).

**2026 currency flags.** ⚠ The "10 %" threshold is the firm's practical reading, not statutory text; label it as such. ⚠ Verify current case law.

**What CTL OS does with it.** Notice-amount check at intake (F-10); late-fee credit calculator feeding the nonpayment defense on the Start phase; curriculum item for Part 2 "Nonpayment of Rent".
