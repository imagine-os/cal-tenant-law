# Security deposits

> **Not verified legal advice.** Written 2026-09-18 from the firm's site as indexed and general knowledge; every citation below has `verified_on: null` in `../statute-index.md`. Verify before use (see `../README.md`).

**What the firm covers.** Recovering deposits after move-out; "all deposits are refundable"; the Security Deposit Recovery Kit (SKU 045: small-claims forms and instructions; site copy "goal is 3x the deposit"); articles /security-deposits/ and /recovering-your-security-deposit.

**Statutes.** CC §1950.5 (refundable, 21-day itemised statement, allowed deductions, pre-move-out inspection), §1950.5(c) (deposit cap), §1950.5(l) (bad-faith penalty), small-claims jurisdiction (CCP §116.220, verify limits).

**2026 currency flags.** ⚠ **AB 12** capped deposits at one month's rent from 2024-07-01 (small-landlord exception); the kit and articles predate it. ⚠ The statutory bad-faith penalty is **up to twice the deposit plus actual damages**, not "3x"; the kit's framing must be reconciled with the statute before it appears in the cost model or plain-language pages. ⚠ Verify current small-claims limits.

**What CTL OS does with it.** Topic page in K-10; deposit demand-letter template (SKU 110 "Simple Letter to Landlord") and small-claims packet as templates; C-30 cost calendar for the deposit path; curriculum items flagged for currency.
