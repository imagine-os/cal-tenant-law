> **Not verified legal advice.** Written 2026-09-18 from the firm's site as indexed and general knowledge; every citation below has `verified_on: null` in `../statute-index.md`. Verify before use (see `../README.md`).

**What the firm covers.** Commercial Tenancies article: rent abatement for structural defects affecting the business; commercial rent control banned statewide since 1987; commercial UD follows the same board with fewer tenant protections.

**Statutes.** CC §1995.010 et seq. (assignment / sublet), CC §1938 (CASp disclosure), CC §1946.1 and CC §827 as modified for qualified commercial tenants by SB 1103 (2024), CCP §1161 et seq. (UD applies), Cal. Rules of Court for limited vs unlimited civil by amount.

**2026 currency flags.** ⚠ **SB 1103** (2025-01-01) created protections for qualified commercial tenants (notice, fees, translations); the firm's article predates it. ⚠ Verify the 1987 rent-control ban citation.

**What CTL OS does with it.** Matter type "commercial" toggles the protections the engine applies; templates for commercial demand and abatement letters; cost bands for unlimited-civil filings.
