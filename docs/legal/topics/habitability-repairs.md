# Habitability and repairs

> **Not verified legal advice.** Written 2026-09-18 from the firm's site as indexed and general knowledge; every citation below has `verified_on: null` in `../statute-index.md`. Verify before use (see `../README.md`).

**What the firm covers.** Implied warranty of habitability; repair and deduct; rent withholding; cockroaches ("Landlords love to blame cockroach infestation on the tenants"), toxic mold, temporary relocation during remediation; breaking a lease for uninhabitable conditions (constructive eviction); habitability as a defense in nonpayment cases.

**Statutes.** CC §1941 / §1941.1 (duty, untenantable conditions), CC §1942 (repair and deduct), CC §1942.4 (no rent demand while cited), CCP §1174.2 (habitability defense in UD), Green v. Superior Court (1974), Health & Safety Code §17920.3 (substandard, mold), CC §1941.7 (mold), CC §1951.2 (mitigation when a lease is broken).

**2026 currency flags.** ⚠ **Repair-and-deduct wording**: the site says "up to two months' rent"; the statute allows up to **one month's** rent, no more than twice in 12 months. Reconcile before the rule appears in client-facing pages. ⚠ Verify current mold / code-enforcement provisions.
