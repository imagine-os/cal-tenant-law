## How the menu works

1. **Free layer first.** Free advice articles, the YouTube channel and the "free legal pre-consultation videos" page ("rewatch and save them for free rather than paying a lawyer for the same information"), free downloads (Game Board PDF, habitability worksheet, PJCRP, small-claims complaint), and the Game Board flow chart that shows "where we are and how long things take".
2. **Intake form → 101.** New clients fill out the Initial Consultation Form (situation, goals, questions in depth). On submit they get a link to the services page and an emailed copy of their answers, then pay **101 ($165)** by card/PayPal and get a phone/Teams slot ("only with a licensed attorney… no paralegals"; all 58 counties).
3. **Vending machine.** In the consult the attorney names the next SKU(s). "For almost all of the actual services there is a set price — like a vending machine, you pay for X and you get X." Documents arrive as PDF; the client files/serves (or buys 065). Items marked *minimum* can trigger a request for a **Supplemental Payment (800–820)**, which the client must not use unless instructed. Anything else is $330/hr.
4. **Stage navigation.** The same products are re-listed under the **Game Board Services** category and under each stage category (Motion to Quash → Default → Discovery by Us / by Them → Demurrer → Answer → Trial Preparation → Settling → Judgment → Appeal → Suing the Landlord), plus contextual sub-categories ("I'm Being Evicted", "If you filed a Motion to Quash…", "If you filed a Demurrer…", "The Discovery Phase", "The Basic Discovery Package", "Our discovery sent to them", "Heading to Trial", "Eviction Defense", "Sue Your Landlord").
5. **Returning-client path.** Follow-up Consultation Form → **102 ($165)**; a separate store view `/paid-legal-services-returning-client/` lists the stage documents directly. The shopping cart's optional registration keeps the client's purchase history ("accounting is electronic").
6. **Hotline for the in-between.** Existing clients with an emergency or one quick question use the VoiceStamps hotline at $60 per 10 minutes instead of a full consult.
7. **Kits as the self-help floor.** $20–$120 PDF kits are positioned as "an introduction to understand the process", and the Trial Kit as the fallback when a tenant goes to trial alone.
