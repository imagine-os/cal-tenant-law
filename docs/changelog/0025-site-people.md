Jeremy Cook renders as initials. Actions `site.filterAttorneys`, `site.openAttorneyPage`.
- **P-06 `/site/videos`**: the 36 videos from `lessons` (`kind = video`) in the site's own three series plus the embedded-only three, thumbnails falling through bundled scrape → firm URL → YouTube still → glyph, duration on every card, search over title / series / presenter, and an in-place `youtube-nocookie` player — created on click, one at a time, no autoplay, focus moved into it. - **P-01**: a "Meet the attorneys" strip (four PersonCards, badged, linking to P-05), a "Watch the free videos" strip under the curriculum, each office card now names its attorney and links to `/site/attorneys?office=<slug>`, and the lesson cards' "play" Placeholder is gone — they open the real library. New actions `site.openAttorneys` and `site.openVideos`; `site.watchVideo` is live instead of a stub.
- **Role scoping (D-048)**: every link in the site module was checked against `getRoutes()`. `/plan` was the only staff-only target: the proposal's "open the project board" card and its `site.openPlan` action (now declared with `permission: 'projects.read'`, which only super admin and owner hold) and the roadmap's plan button render only for those roles; everyone else keeps the roadmap, which embeds the same plan data. - **P-02 mapping**: `planFor()` now also resolves page codes that no task carries in its own `code` field, through a small `CODE_TASKS` table (P-05 → T-133, P-06 → T-130 / T-078, P-11 / P-12 / P-13 → T-079).
