## What landed

- **L-13 `/counsel/pipeline`** (attorney, paralegal, owner, super admin; nav Documents & filings, first). Four header stats (in progress, waiting on clients with the longest wait named, late, due this week). Three views in the query string: **Board** — one column per main-track stage under six headings from the stage `kind` (Intake, Drafting, Client review, Approval, Filing & service, Done), with on hold and cancelled in a collapsed row; **Table** — the same rows as a sortable DataTable; **Waiting on clients** — longest wait first with each order's open requests, when they were asked and on which channel, and a Nudge. Filters (Mine · All, waiting on, document kind, late only, client search) are all in the URL. Attorneys default to Mine.
- **L-14 `/counsel/orders/:orderId`**. Header with DocPreview, both stage labels, the wait, the due and filing dates. Actions bar of nine transitions, each `applyTransition`. StageStepper across the whole main track with the collapsed client wording under every internal stage. Five tabs: Timeline (append-only events with who, when, note and waiting-on-after), Client requests (+ an ask form that writes the request and the desk's follow-up, and offers the move to gathering_client_details), Documents (drafting-studio link, filed copies, upload Placeholder), Details (court, case number, board square, assignments, staff notes, the client-facing summary — all saved by id), Activity (calls and follow-ups on the order).
- **S-13 `/assist/queue`**. Four sections by what is needed from the paralegal — gather, prepare, file and serve, and a read-only "waiting on the client" — each card carrying the one move that usually comes next as a single button.
- **C-11 `/app/orders` + C-11a `/app/orders/:orderId`**. "Waiting on you (N)" at the top, a loud block per order listing the open requests, a client stepper with dates, "what happens next" per stage, the draft as a large DocPreview in client review with Approve and Request changes, a Textarea answer for questions and a link to the binder upload for items. Spanish complete.
- **F-14 `/desk/orders`**. Instant search over name, phone, order ref, title and case number; a results table showing the staff stage and the client wording side by side; a Drawer status card with the three-line phone script, the open requests, Log this call (writes a `calls` row and stamps `last_client_touch_at`) and Add a follow-up; Move-to disabled with a Tooltip naming who can.
- **Components** `WaitingOnPill`, `StageStepper`, `OrderCard`, each with a `.meta.ts` and usages (D-02 picks them up).
- **Module plumbing** `useOrders(filter)` (tenant-scoped rows plus events, requests, follow-ups, calls, users and cases with name and lookup helpers) and the pure `orderCardModel(order, events, now)`; `useAdvance()` and `useNudge()` are the only write paths for staff.
