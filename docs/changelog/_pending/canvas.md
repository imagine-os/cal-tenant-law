## What was built

**The surface.** One `translate(...) scale(zoom)` on a world div with the windows absolutely positioned in world pixels, so a pan or a zoom is a single composited transform. Zoom 0.04–2 by buttons, `+` / `−`, the wheel anchored on the cursor (Shift+wheel pans), two-finger pinch, 100 %, Fit all, Fit selection and `F`. Grid-dot background, a minimap that draws every window plus the viewport rectangle, and the arrow keys panning when no window has focus.

**The window** (`BrowserWindow` organism). Traffic-light dots, page-code chip, page name, role / language / device chips, inspector / duplicate / close buttons, an editable URL bar showing `#/path` (Enter navigates that frame, Escape restores), the live page as the body, and a footer handle showing the path and the size. Draggable by the title bar, resizable from all eight handles (minimum 320×240), both committed in a `requestAnimationFrame`. Devices: phone 390×844, tablet 768×1024, laptop 1280×800, desktop 1920×1080, TV 3840×2160 at half size; the page renders at the device viewport and is transform-scaled into the body, so it keeps its real layout. A transparent capture layer covers the body while a gesture is running and whenever the zoom is under 0.5; clicking it (or Enter) enters the window and hands the page the pointer. Under 0.5 the per-window buttons and the address field are hidden and out of the tab order, because a 7 px target is a lie.

**Keyboard parity.** A focused window takes arrows (move 16 px), Shift+arrows (resize), Alt+arrows (snap to the nearest neighbour edge), `I` (inspector), Enter (enter), Delete (remove); the inspector Drawer exposes X, Y, width, height, device preset, role, language and address as inputs, plus Bring to front, Duplicate, Zoom to, Open the full page and Close. Selection is click / Shift+click; Delete removes with an 8-second undo bar.

**Layouts.** Presets: "All pages by surface" (the old shelf packing, generalised, kept as pure maths in `canvasLayout.ts` with its labelled group boxes), "Per role" (one row per surface the role can reach, from each route's own `roles` array, D-048), "Pipeline walkthrough" (the seeded `cvl_pipeline_walkthrough` row: L-13, L-14, C-11 in Spanish on a phone, F-12, F-14 — placed even where a path is not built yet, the frame then showing whatever the route really renders). Save / Load / Rename / Delete write `canvas_layouts` through the `DataProvider` by id (`viewport`, `windows[]`, `flows_visible`, `role_filter`, `owner_user_id`); the working surface also persists per viewer in `localStorage ctl.canvas.v2`.

**The second level** (`FlowLayer` organism + `canvasFlows.ts`). "Show flows" plus a role Select draws `ROLE_FLOWS[role]` in world coordinates: a page step on the first window whose `code` matches, a page step with no window as a dashed ghost node with an "Add window" button beside its predecessor, and action / decision / hand-off steps as pills / diamonds / role tags beside the window of the page they happen on. Edges are cubic curves in the KEY colours (`--board-normal/positive/negative-fg`, hand-off in the jump colour), with labels at the midpoints, arrowheads, and the target role named on a cross-role hand-off. A legend sits in the corner in screen space so it stays legible at any zoom. "Step through" walks the flow (buttons and `←` / `→`): the step's window glows, the rest dim, a caption bar names the step, Fit selection moves the view, Escape leaves. "Focus on this role" hides every window that role's flow does not touch.

**Performance.** Live iframes capped (default 8; 4 / 8 / 12 / 20), chosen by distance from the viewport centre once the view settles at 180 ms, plus whatever was entered or loaded by hand; wireframe tiles (`BrandArt variant="window"`) beyond the cap and under 0.2 zoom; the window wrapper is memoised and the iframe `src` only changes when the path, role, language, theme or builder-tool flag does, so dragging and panning never reload a page.
